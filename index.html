AI JOURNALING · RESPONSIBLE AI

Mana — AI Memory & Journaling Companion

Mana is an AI-powered journaling and personal-reflection companion designed to help people turn memories, thoughts, and personal experiences into meaningful written reflections. It goes beyond a basic journal through guided writing and thoughtful AI assistance, while respecting user autonomy, privacy, and clear ethical boundaries. Mana is not a therapist, a diagnostic system, or a replacement for professional care.

Key design decisions

  • Faithful memory: Mana never attributes a memory the user did not write.
  • User autonomy: it supports the user's own thinking instead of deciding for them.
  • Questions over advice: instead of interpreting, it asks, for example, “Which part of this made you feel misunderstood?”
  • Clear boundary: a reflection and journaling tool, not therapy or diagnosis.

Cognitive UX Assistant

An AI assistant concept combining user-experience principles with cognitive psychology to help people clarify what they need, organize their ideas, and shape more useful results. It considers factors such as attention, cognitive load, clarity, and user control.

AI Subtitle & Language Companion

A developing tool concept to help viewers understand films and series in languages they do not speak by providing subtitles in their own language. The translation and subtitle experience still need refinement, and improving accuracy and natural phrasing remains part of the work.

AI Private Tutor & Study Assistant

An AI learning companion designed to support students with explanations, study guidance, and help working through academic topics. Its aim is to adapt support to the learner's needs and encourage understanding, progress, and independent learning—not simply provide answers.
